Dodge - Privacy Policy

Developer: Copper Haven Studios

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Information We Collect

Dodge does not require accounts or ask players for personal information.

The current version does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

Game Progress

Dodge saves game progress, including scores, coins, and unlocked items, locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to Copper Haven Studios.

Data Sharing

Copper Haven Studios does not sell or share personal information collected by Dodge. Google Play may process information independently when you download or use its services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress remains on your device until the application's data is cleared or the game is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

Dodge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Dodge's data practices change, this privacy policy will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: copperhavenstudios@gmail.com
